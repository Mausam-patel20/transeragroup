Kapil Shah is the Founder of Transera Group Ltd., a Canadian electrification advisory firm specializing in owner-side EPCM coordination for EV charging and electrical infrastructure. With 20+ years of field-led delivery and exposure to $100M+ portfolios, Kapil bridges trades expertise (Master Electrician, C.Tech, Licensed Electrician) and program governance (PMP, MBA-Sustainability in progress).
